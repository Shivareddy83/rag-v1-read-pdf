# LinkedIn Technology Post — AI Automation

AI automation is moving beyond chatbots.

The bigger opportunity is connecting AI to the workflows businesses already use.

A lead arrives → AI classifies it → relevant information is extracted → the lead is routed → a message is generated → the team gets notified.

The value is not simply “using AI.”

The value is removing repetitive steps while keeping people in control of decisions that actually require judgment.

For developers, this means the next generation of AI applications will need more than prompts. They will need APIs, databases, workflow orchestration, observability, security, and reliable integrations.

AI becomes useful when it becomes part of the system, not just a demo.
