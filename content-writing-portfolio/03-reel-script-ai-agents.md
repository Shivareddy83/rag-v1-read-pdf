# Short-form Reel Script — AI Agents Explained

**Hook (0–5 sec):**  
“What if an AI could do more than answer your question?”

**Body (5–35 sec):**  
“An AI agent can take a goal, decide what steps are needed, use tools, and work through those steps. For example, instead of only telling you that a customer submitted a lead form, an agent could read the lead, classify the request, update a CRM, prepare a response, and notify the sales team.

That is the difference between an AI that only generates text and an AI system that can actually participate in a workflow.”

**Close (35–45 sec):**  
“The future of AI isn't just better answers. It's AI connected to real systems and real actions.”
