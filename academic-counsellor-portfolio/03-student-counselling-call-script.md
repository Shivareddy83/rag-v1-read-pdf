# Student Counselling Call Script — Work Sample

## Opening
“Hi, I’m Shiva. I’d like to understand what you are currently studying, what career direction you are considering, and what kind of learning support you are looking for.”

## Discovery
- “What are you currently studying?”
- “What career options are you considering?”
- “What skills have you already developed?”
- “What is the biggest challenge you are facing right now?”
- “What would you like to achieve in the next 3–6 months?”

## Understanding & Confirmation
“Let me summarize what I understood: your current goal is ___, your current level is ___, and your main constraint is ___. Is that correct?”

## Program Explanation
“Based on what you shared, I can explain the programs that are relevant to your goal, including eligibility, duration, learning outcomes, and support.”

## Closing
“Do you have any questions about the program or the next steps? I can summarize the information so you can review it before making a decision.”

**Skills demonstrated:** communication, active listening, requirement discovery, clear explanation, problem-solving, and structured follow-up.

**This is a self-created counselling work sample, not a record of real counselling activity.**
