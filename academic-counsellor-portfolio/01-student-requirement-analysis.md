# Student Requirement Analysis — Work Sample

**Purpose:** Demonstrate structured listening, requirement discovery, and problem-solving for an Academic Counsellor role.

## Student Profile (Scenario)
A first/second-year student is unsure which learning program to choose and is concerned about career direction.

## Discovery Questions
1. What is your current course, year, and academic background?
2. What career areas are you currently considering?
3. What subjects or skills do you enjoy most?
4. What is your current level of practical experience?
5. What is your weekly time availability?
6. What outcome do you expect from a learning program?
7. What constraints should be considered — budget, schedule, location, or prerequisites?

## Requirement Summary
After listening, summarize the student's goals, current level, constraints, and desired outcome before suggesting any program.

## Guidance Principle
Do not recommend a program only because it is popular. Match the student's stated goal, current level, requirements, and constraints to the available opportunity.

**This is a self-created counselling work sample, not a record of real counselling activity.**
