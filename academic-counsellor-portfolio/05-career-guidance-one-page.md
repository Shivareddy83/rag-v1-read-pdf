# Career Guidance One-Page — Work Sample

## A simple framework for guiding a student

**1. Understand**  
Identify the student's academic background, interests, goals, current skills, and constraints.

**2. Clarify**  
Separate the student's immediate learning need from their longer-term career goal.

**3. Explain**  
Present relevant programs or opportunities in simple language, including eligibility, learning outcomes, duration, and next steps.

**4. Compare**  
Explain meaningful differences between options instead of overwhelming the student with information.

**5. Confirm**  
Ask the student to summarize what they understood and resolve remaining questions.

**6. Follow up**  
Record the agreed next action and follow-up date.

## Communication standard
Be clear, respectful, patient, and student-focused. The purpose of counselling is to help the student understand their options and make an informed decision.

**This is a self-created guidance framework, not a claim of professional counselling experience.**
